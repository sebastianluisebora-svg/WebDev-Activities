MAIN DISH · EASY

Chicken Adobo

Tender chicken simmered in a savory, tangy soy-vinegar sauce.

◷ 45 min♧ Serves 4

You'll need

1 kg chicken thighs, 1/2 cup soy sauce, 1/3 cup vinegar, 1 head crushed garlic, 3 bay leaves, 1 tsp whole peppercorns

Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes. Simmer with vinegar, bay leaves, and peppercorns until tender, then reduce the sauce.

SOUP · MEDIUM

Sinigang na Baboy

Pork ribs and vegetables in a bright, comforting sour broth.

◷ 70 min♧ Serves 5

You'll need

1 kg pork ribs, 1 pack sinigang mix or fresh tamarind, 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 1 radish (labanos), 1 bunch kangkong, fish sauce to taste

Boil pork with onion and tomatoes until tender. Add the sour base and radish, then the greens. Season with fish sauce.

MAIN DISH · SLOW COOK

Kare-Kare

A rich peanut stew with tender beef and garden vegetables.

◷ 2 hr 30 min♧ Serves 6

You'll need

1 kg oxtail or beef shank, 1/2 cup peanut butter, 1/4 cup ground toasted rice, 1 eggplant, 1 bunch bok choy, string beans, bagoong (shrimp paste) for serving

Boil the beef until very tender. Stir in peanut butter and ground rice to thicken the broth. Add vegetables and serve with bagoong.

NOODLES · EASY

Pancit Canton

A quick, colorful noodle stir-fry for family gatherings.

◷ 30 min♧ Serves 4

You'll need

250 g pancit canton noodles, 200 g sliced chicken, 1 thinly sliced carrot, 1 cup cabbage, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, calamansi for serving

Saute garlic, onion, and chicken. Add vegetables, sauces, and a cup of broth. Toss in the noodles until they absorb the liquid.

DESSERT · MEDIUM

Leche Flan

Silky steamed custard finished with a glossy caramel top.

◷ 90 min♧ Serves 8

You'll need

10 egg yolks, 1 can condensed milk, 1 can evaporated milk, 1 tsp vanilla, 3/4 cup sugar for caramel

Caramelize the sugar in the mold. Mix the remaining ingredients, strain, pour in, and steam for 45 minutes. Chill before unmolding.

DESSERT · NO COOK

Halo-Halo

Shaved ice layered with sweet Filipino treats and creamy milk.

◷ 20 min♧ Serves 2

You'll need

2 cups shaved ice, sweet beans and nata de coco, sweetened banana (saba), 1 slice leche flan, ube halaya, evaporated milk

Layer the sweet ingredients in a tall glass. Pack with shaved ice, pour evaporated milk, and top with ube and flan.
